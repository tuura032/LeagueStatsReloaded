About the league

Fantasy Football Fantasy is a 12-team ESPN fantasy football league that started in 2015. This site computes the league's dual-point standings: each week an owner earns one point for winning their head-to-head matchup and a second for finishing in the top half of the league in points, so the table rewards scoring as well as winning. ESPN doesn't compute that, which is why this site exists. Membership changed in the first few years and has been stable since.

Draft

An auction draft with a ${{ rules.draft.budget }} budget per team and a {{ (rules.draft.clockSeconds / 60) | int }}-minute clock on each selection. The draft is held the day after Labor Day (typically a Tuesday or Wednesday).

Keepers

Each team may carry over {{ rules.keepers.count }} keepers into the next season. Keeper values are set in the auction: a keeper's base price rises each year they're retained: +$7 in year one, +$10 in year two, +$12 in year three. Undrafted free agents (UDFAs) start at a $5 base.

Playoffs

The top {{ rules.playoffs.teamCount }} teams make the playoffs. Playoff games carry a +{{ rules.playoffs.homeTeamBonus }}-point home-field bonus. Seeding is by total points scored, but the league reseeds by hand off the dual-point standings rather than trusting ESPN's automatic seed.

Tiebreakers

Playoff seeding is by total points scored. Because the league reseeds the bracket by hand off the dual-point standings, the seed order on the standings page is the one that decides the bracket.

Trades & FAAB

Trades are allowed all season and require {{ rules.trades.vetoVotesRequired }} veto votes to block. Free-agent acquisition is traditional FAAB with a ${{ rules.faab.budget }} budget. The trade deadline is set by the commissioner each season.

Dues & Payouts

The entry fee is ${{ entryFee | int }} per team. The full pot and how it's split are on the Playoffs and Prizes page.
